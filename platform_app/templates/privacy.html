Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ngotra AI ("Ngotra", "we", "us") collects when you use our website at ngotra.site, our developer dashboard, and our OpenAI-compatible API gateway (collectively, the "Service"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently.

At a glance

  • • We collect only what's needed to run accounts, usage billing, and the API gateway.
  • • If you connect a Gemini account, the session is encrypted at rest and used only for your requests.
  • • We never sell your personal data.
  • • You can request deletion of your account and data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: email address, password hash, account role, and registration date when you create an account.
  • API keys & tokens: generated API keys, token balance, token grants, and token usage used to operate the service.
  • Usage logs: request endpoint, model used, status code, response time, estimated token count, and IP address associated with API requests.
  • Connected Gemini account: if you choose to connect your Google/Gemini account through our optional connector, your Gemini session credentials are stored encrypted and used only to route your API requests.
  • Site settings & preferences: configuration stored in your browser session (such as a login token in an HTTP-only cookie).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account, authenticate you, and secure access to the dashboard.
  • Operate the API gateway, enforce token balances, and measure usage.
  • Route your requests to Gemini when you have connected your own account.
  • Detect abuse, prevent fraud, and keep the Service reliable and secure.
  • Communicate important service updates and respond to your support requests.

We do not use your personal data for targeted advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Connected Gemini Accounts

Connecting your Gemini account is entirely optional. If you use our browser connector, it reads your signed-in Gemini session from your browser with your permission and sends it to our servers. The session is encrypted at rest (Fernet encryption) and is used only to make Gemini API requests on your behalf. Your API access is blocked until a working connection is verified.

You can disconnect at any time from Dashboard → Settings. Disconnecting removes the stored session and immediately stops API access for your account. A Gemini session grants access to your Google account; only connect an account you are comfortable using with this Service.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use a single HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in to the dashboard. This cookie is essential for authentication and cannot be used for tracking. Our sponsored/ad partners (Adsterra) and analytics may set their own cookies when you view ads or visit embedded content; those are governed by their own privacy policies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The Service interacts with third parties to function:

  • Google / Gemini: when you connect an account, requests are sent to Google's servers to generate responses. Google's Privacy Policy applies to that interaction.
  • Adsterra: we display sponsored content to fund free access. Ad interactions are handled by Adsterra and subject to their policies.
  • Hosting & infrastructure: the Service is hosted on servers that store the data described above.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

6. Data Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. Usage logs are retained for a limited period (typically 30 days) for security and troubleshooting, then removed. API keys remain active until you revoke them. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Contact).

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted storage of connected session credentials, HTTP-only authentication cookies, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Revoke API keys and disconnect connected accounts at any time.
  • Withdraw consent where consent was the basis for processing.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Service or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
